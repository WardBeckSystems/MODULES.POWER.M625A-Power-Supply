## 490E module Which power suppy to use M624 or M625A

_WBS RACKMOUNTING · Oct 15, 2013|46_

> I have some 490E Line modules that I am racking. Which power supply is correct to use :The M624 or the M625A?

— **Blueroom1**, Oct 15, 2013|46

> Hi.
>
> as the documentation indicates the M624 is an un-regulated supply giving you only +24v - http://wbsps.ca/PDFs/Ward-Beck%20Systems%20-%20M624%20-%20Power%20Supply%20-%20www.WBSps.ca.pdf - you require a bi-polar supply for WBS modules. You could force this one to work, but being un-regulated you are susceptible to noise form the electrical supply. Please use the significantly larger M625 series units. They are intended to be dead quiet, fire up an entire console, and work around the clock. The M624 is a little supply intended for bulbs in the CH ON, meters, relays, etc...
>
> Hope this helps a little bit.
>
> Regards,
>
> Warren

— **warrenbeck**, Oct 16, 2013|46

> Thank you . I don't have a M625a so I will have to order one of the JLM Kits and build a regulated supply.
